About

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from KFUPM focused on AI infrastructure. My background spans networking, cloud, IoT and machine learning. During my internship in Aramco's IT Engineering Department, I worked on enterprise routers and switches, load balancer design and data center connectivity.

During my studies, I also gained research experience at the SDAIA–KFUPM Joint Research Center on Indoor Radar-Based Human Activity Recognition, using CNNs, Transformers and explainable AI.

After graduating, I joined Saudi Digital Academy's AI Data Center Operations program. There I deployed open-weight LLMs on a GPU Kubernetes cluster, monitored them with Prometheus and Grafana, and benchmarked their accuracy and latency.

Projects

LLM Serving Platform on Kubernetes

Built during the Saudi Digital Academy AI Data Center Operations Bootcamp. An OpenAI-compatible FastAPI model service in Docker, deployed with Helm and autoscaling, and vLLM serving Llama 3.1 and Qwen 2.5 on a GPU Kubernetes node, monitored with Prometheus and Grafana. On top of it, a team benchmark of the self-hosted models against gpt-4o-mini on English and Arabic RFPs.

View repository

SafeVein AR: Near-Infrared Vein Detection Glasses

Senior design project with a six-person team. AI-powered AR glasses that find the safest hand vein for an IV line. A deep learning segmentation model (U-Net) detects veins in near-infrared video, quantized to INT8 and run on edge AI hardware (Raspberry Pi 5 with a Hailo-8 accelerator). An intelligent decision support system then ranks the veins and picks the insertion point, with about 60 ms end-to-end latency.

Indoor Radar-Based Human Activity Recognition

Research at the SDAIA–KFUPM Joint Research Center on radar-based fall detection, applying explainable AI with CNNs and Vision Transformers.

Hospital Appointments System on OpenStack

Cloud computing course project. Deployed a hospital appointments web app on a private OpenStack cloud using Keystone, Glance, Nova, Neutron, Cinder and Horizon. The web and database servers run on separate VMs and networks, with security groups keeping the database off the internet.

Smart Office Access with Face Recognition

IoT course project. An ESP32 camera sends face images over MQTT to a local server for recognition, and a second ESP32 opens the door and switches office power. I built the camera node, the face recognition model and the MQTT broker, and measured network latency and protocol overhead.

Robbery Alert System

Embedded systems course project. An anti-theft alarm on an mbed LPC1768 (ARM Cortex-M3) that locks with a keypad password and sounds a buzzer and LED when an accelerometer detects movement. Written in C++, with most peripherals driven directly through registers.

View repository

Vowel Recognition with Machine Learning

Machine learning course project. Classifies 11 English vowel sounds from speech features, comparing KNN, SVMs and a deep neural network. The neural network reached 99% test accuracy, and tests on unseen speakers measured how well each model generalizes.
